04 HOW WE ASSESS PURCHASES

Look closer.
See the whole transaction.

Our purchasing approach considers:

  1. 01

    Condition & history

    Reviewing the vehicle’s condition, available records and potential repair requirements.

  2. 02

    Ownership & liens

    Checking ownership documentation and identifying outstanding liens that need to be addressed.

  3. 03

    Market demand

    Assessing potential resale demand and pricing using dealer contacts and available market information.

  4. 04

    Total costs

    Accounting for transportation, repairs, auction fees, currency exchange and other applicable transaction costs.

  5. 05

    Purchase economics

    Comparing estimated sale proceeds with the total cost of acquiring and reselling the vehicle.

Resale prices, expenses and timelines can change. Each transaction carries commercial risk.

05 OUR STORY

Practical experience.
A deeper perspective.

8

years of auto-carrier drivingPart of our founders’ experience

We’re building True Depth Capital around a shared belief: good buying decisions start with understanding the details.

On the road. At the table.

Our combined experience includes driving an auto carrier for 8 years and transporting thousands of vehicles, primarily to the United States, alongside negotiating agreements with landowners and industry representatives in the oilfield.

That background brings practical knowledge of cross-border vehicle transportation and experience working through concerns, understanding different perspectives and reaching agreements.

Planning. Coordination. Improvement.

We also bring experience in management, technology, supply chain management and quality improvement. We’re applying that background to how we plan transportation, coordinate resources, track costs and review purchasing decisions.

Together, we’re building a business focused on sourcing vehicles in Canada for resale to licensed U.S. dealers. Our approach combines practical transportation knowledge and negotiation experience with careful planning and a clear understanding of each transaction’s costs and risks.
